<objective>
Add a third player-side healthbox tile tag and override healthbox positions for RAID battles so all three ally healthboxes stack vertically on the right side of the screen without VRAM conflicts.

Purpose: The 3v1 layout has battler 3 at B_POSITION_OPPONENT_RIGHT but on the player side. The engine only has two player-side healthbox templates (indices 0 and 1); battlers 2 and 3 both resolve to index 1, causing a VRAM tile collision. This plan fixes the collision and repositions all four healthboxes for the raid layout.

Output: Modified `battle_interface.h` (new constant) and `battle_interface.c` (new template entry + two RAID guards).
</objective>
